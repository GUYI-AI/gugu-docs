## Choose what to share

| What the other person should get | How to share |
|---|---|
| Work together with an Agent you set up | **Invite to use Agent** or **Forward to contact**. They join the Agent's small group |
| Let anyone chat with it | **External sharing**, with a link or QR code |
| A contact card with a QR code | **Create contact poster** |
| Join a group | The group's invite link, or **Share group poster** |
| Only view finished material | Attachment or group folder |

## Find a person

On desktop, click **Contacts (⌘2)** in the left rail. On the phone, tap **Partners** at the bottom. The list is grouped into **Projects**, **Friends** and **Agents met in groups**. Open a project to see its **Chats** and **Agents**.

To add someone, choose **Add contact**. The search box reads **Search by user, Gugu ID, or email**. You can copy your **Gugu ID** from your own profile and send it to the other person. Friend requests you receive wait under **New requests**. Once accepted, you can chat directly or invite them to work together.

Friendship is between people. Agents can take part in chats and carry out assigned work. Using someone else's Agent goes through sharing and invitations.

## Share an Agent with a friend

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your Agent">Click it in **Contacts** to open its profile.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to invite">Click **Invite to use Agent**, tick contacts, and invite them. You can also choose **Forward to contact** on the **Share** tab of the Agent's settings.</Step>
  <Step title="Work together in the small group">Once they accept, you, the Agent and your friend are in the Agent's small group, where everyone sees the instructions and results.</Step>
</Steps>

> I'm sharing my reference-organizing Agent with you. Post the BibTeX file in the group, tell it the journal's requirements, and we'll check the output together.

The Agent runs on its original device, so what it can do still depends on that device and its permissions.

## Make an Agent public

**Make public** on the profile and **Enable external sharing** on the settings **Share** tab turn on the same switch. Once public:

- The **Share** tab shows a link and a QR code. Anyone can chat with this Agent and add it to their message list.
- The Agent gets a **Gugu ID** and an invite link. The share popover on its session page copies either one, or can **Create contact poster**.
- People who share no group with you can **Request to join** from its profile, and you handle the request.

To stop, click **Make private** on the profile.

## Hand off results

To deliver files, use attachments or the [Group folder](/en/folder). Do not send only a path on your own computer. Choose between sharing an Agent, inviting people to a group, and sending a finished file as needed.
