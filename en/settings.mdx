On desktop, the gear at the bottom of the far-left column opens Settings. On macOS you can also press `⌘ ,`. On the phone, tap your avatar at the top left of **Messages** or **Partners** to open **Me**.

## Find a setting

Settings come in five groups. The desktop's left column and the phone's **Me** use the same groups.

| Group | Pages | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Gugu** | **Profile**, **Connected apps** | The personal assistant's name, look, and status; the [apps](/en/integrations) it can use |
| **Me** | **Account**, **Usage**, **File storage**, **Subscriptions** | Edit your profile, sign out; see credit usage; choose where project group files are kept and for how long; manage membership and provider connections |
| **Preferences** | **General**, **Interface**, **Permissions**, **Models**, **Resource monitor** | Theme, language, notifications, terminal, tray badge; sidebar and keyboard shortcuts; system permissions; which models appear in the model menu; [Resource monitor](/en/resource-monitor) |
| **Work** | **Tasks**, **Browser**, **Devices** | Defaults for new tasks and agent notifications; browser profiles, each keeping its own cookies and logins; adding and managing the machines that run Agents |
| **About** | **About** | Current version, update check, what's new, feedback |

When **Terminal** in **General** is on, agents can interact through a terminal interface. New tasks still default to the regular mode.

On the phone, **Me** has no **Tasks**, **Interface**, **Browser**, **Subscriptions**, or **Resource monitor** pages. The **Work** group adds two pages: **Apps** manages the Apps installed on this phone, and **This phone** manages phone tasks and permissions. See [Mobile](/en/mobile).

Settings related to tools on a machine may require choosing a device first.

## Updates and versions

The **stable release** suits stable everyday use. **Beta** gets new features first. The personal assistant, cross-device projects, and recent App actions in these docs follow the current Beta. The stable release's interface may differ.

Check the current version in **About**. When a desktop update has downloaded, an update entry appears at the bottom of the left column. Click it to restart and install. On Android, download the installer as the app prompts. Save anything you are editing before updating.

The stable release only updates to stable releases, and Beta only to Beta. You can install both at once. Each keeps its own app data.

For recent changes, see the [release notes](https://gugu.nextmind.space/help/en/release-notes/macos/).

## Membership and purchases

The client shows your current entitlements and usage. Manage membership, renew, buy top-up packs, and view purchase history in the [membership dashboard](https://gugu.nextmind.space/dashboard/). Prices are shown on the checkout page.

For model sources and credit validity, see [Models, subscriptions, and credits](/en/models).
