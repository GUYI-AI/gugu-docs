## Send messages and files

In desktop chat, press Enter to send and Shift + Enter for a new line. Unsent text stays as a draft. Paste or pick files and images, then say what to do with them.

In a group, use `@` to name a member. To continue earlier work, reply to the original message. Material the Agent needs to see should be sent as an attachment or an accessible link.

## Search a chat

Groups and topics have a magnifier in the header, **Search this chat**. On the phone it sits under **More actions**. In a private chat, choose **Search chat** under **More actions**.

## Mark up an image

Open an image in the chat and choose **Edit image**. The tools are Rectangle, Arrow, Number, Pen, Text, Pixel mosaic and Crop. Use the mosaic to hide accounts, email addresses or anything else that should not travel further.

Each number can carry a note. Turn on **Include numbered notes** to send the notes with the image.

When you finish, choose where it goes:

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| The send button | Sent back to the source chat as a reply to the original image. The button names the destination. If the original was a whisper, it reads **Whisper to …** and only that person receives it |
| **Put in composer** | Places it in the input box so you can add text first |
| **Done → Save to chat folder** | Saves it to this chat's [Group folder](/en/folder) |
| **Done → Copy image** or **Save to device** | Keeps it for yourself |

An image that did not come from a chat can only be copied or saved to your device.

Choose **Open a topic** in a message's menu to discuss that message on its own. Later messages in the topic only notify its participants.

Topics pinned to the group appear as tabs on the group chat page. The first tab is **Group**. For visibility, pinning and notification rules, see [Topics](/en/topics).

## Whispers

In a group, click a member's avatar to open the profile card and choose **Whisper**. Only you and that member can see it, and the message is labeled **Whisper · only visible to you both**. Conclusions and deliverables the whole team needs to know go back to the group or topic.

## Share with other apps on Android

Long-press a message and choose **Forward → Share to other apps**. The system share sheet hands supported text, images, or files to apps such as WeChat or email.

When you share content to gugu from another app, first pick the chat to send it to. The content goes into the input box, where you can add a request. Nothing is sent until you tap send. If WeChat offers no share option for outside apps, copy the text, or use "Open with another app" on the file.
