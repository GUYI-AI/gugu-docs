## Send messages and files

In desktop chat, press Enter to send and Shift + Enter for a new line. Unsent text stays as a draft. Paste or pick files and images, then say what to do with them.

*Interface animation: open a topic from an existing message and continue the discussion alongside it. The video has built-in Chinese subtitles.*

1. Find the message you want to discuss further, open its message menu, and choose **New topic**.
2. The topic name defaults to the original message. You can change it, then start posting.
3. In the topic, `@` the people or Agents who should take part.

On desktop, the source chat stays open and the topic opens beside it. On the phone, tap into the topic and use the back button at the top left to return to the source chat.

| Question | Rule for regular topics |
|---|---|
| Can others in the group see it? | Yes. The source group can see it and open it to read. |
| Who gets notified of new messages? | Participants: members who posted in it or were mentioned and added. |
| Does a new topic switch to another Agent or working directory? | No. The same Agent keeps working in the same place. |
| An old topic disappeared from the list. What now? | Look for it in the source chat's past topics. It reappears when someone posts in it. |

## Whispers

Start a **Whisper** from a group member's profile card. Only the sender and that one member can see it, and the message is labeled with its scope. Conclusions and deliverables the whole team needs to know go back to the group or topic.

## Share with other apps on Android

Long-press a message and choose **Forward → Share to other apps**. The system share sheet hands supported text, images, or files to apps such as WeChat or email.

When you share content to gugu from another app, first pick the chat to send it to. The content goes into the input box, where you can add a request. Nothing is sent until you tap send. If WeChat offers no share option for outside apps, copy the text, or use "Open with another app" on the file.

## Replies, failures, and retries

While an Agent works, you can follow its progress and read the reply as it arrives. Follow any connection, sign-in, or quota prompts.
