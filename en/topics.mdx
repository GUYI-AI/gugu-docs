A topic grows out of one message and keeps the discussion about it in one place. You can open topics in groups and in private chats. The source chat can see a topic, but later messages only notify its participants.

## Open a topic

1. Find the message you want to discuss further, open its message menu, and choose **Open a topic**.
2. The topic name defaults to the start of the message. Rename it at the top of the **Topic members** list if needed.
3. In the topic, `@` the people or Agents who should take part, and say what needs doing.

On desktop, the topic opens in a split view beside the source chat. **Collapse split view** only hides that pane; the topic stays open. On the phone, the topic fills the screen.

An entry appears under the original message with the message count and number of participants. Click it to go in.

## Who can see it, who gets notified

| Question | Rule |
|---|---|
| Can others in the group see it? | Yes. They can open it and read. |
| Who gets notified of new messages? | Participants: members who posted in it or were mentioned and added |
| What if I am not a participant yet? | Post once in the topic and you become one |
| Does a new topic switch to another Agent or working directory? | No. The same Agent keeps working in the same place. |

## Find every topic

In a group chat header, open **More actions** and go to **Topics**. It lists all topics in the group with their latest time, a preview and unread counts. Pinned topics come first.

## Pin to the group's tabs

In the **Topics** list, click the pin on the right of a topic, **Pin … to group**.

Once something is pinned, a row of tabs appears under the group header. The first tab is **Group**, followed by one tab per pinned topic. Click a tab to switch to that topic, and click **Group** to return. Desktop and phone share the same row of tabs.

Pins only change your own tabs. Others see their own. To remove one, click the same pin, **Unpin …**.

In private chats on the phone, every topic appears as a tab without pinning.

## Tasks in a project

A task you hand to an Agent in a project group opens its own topic from the task card. Pin it, and its tab shows whether the task is running or waiting for you.

## When there are too many Agents

Topics follow the source group's Agent limit. Past that number, all Agents switch to passive and only an `@` mention wakes them. Set the group's limit in **Group management → Agent collaboration**. See [Groups and collaboration](/en/groups#listen-mode).

In a private chat you can set a limit per topic: open **More actions → Conversation info**, pick the topic under **Topic thresholds**, and enter a number. Leave it empty to follow the source chat.
