When you first open gugu, start with the left rail. Almost every entry point begins there.

<Note>This page matches the desktop and Android apps in the current Beta. The stable release may differ; see [Updates and versions](/en/settings#updates-and-versions).</Note>

## The desktop left rail

From top to bottom:

| Icon | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| Gugu avatar | Your [personal assistant](/en/assistant). Before setup, the avatar has a **+** in the corner, and hovering shows a hint such as **Set up your Gugu** |
| **Messages** | Projects, tasks, and chats with people and Agents. On macOS, press ⌘1 |
| **Contacts** | Find people, add friends, and manage your own Agents; see [Contacts and sharing](/en/contacts). On macOS, press ⌘2 |
| **Library** | Keep your go-to **Prompts**, and manage **Agents** and **Integrations** |
| **Apps** | Your installed Apps. Right-click to list the Apps you can open |
| App icons | Apps pinned to the rail. Drag to reorder. Right-click for **Open**, **Open in New Window**, **Take off the left rail** and **Quit**; see [Apps](/en/apps) |
| **Help & tour** | Replay the interface tour |
| **Settings** | Account, models, credits and preferences; see [Settings](/en/settings). On macOS you can also press ⌘, |

Hover over an icon to see its name.

## The two tabs inside Messages

Click **Messages**. The top of the sidebar shows two tabs, **Projects | Chats**. A number on a tab is the unread count.

**The Projects tab**, from top to bottom:

- **New task**: opens the new task page; see [Task workspace](/en/tasks).
- **Task history**: find past tasks, including archived ones. Click **Restore** to put one back in the list.
- **Scheduled**: all scheduled work; see [Schedules](/en/schedules).
- **Pinned**: tasks you pinned.
- **Projects**: one row per project. **Add project** next to the heading creates a project or adds a folder. The **+** on a project row starts a new task in that project; see [Projects and folders](/en/projects).
- **Tasks**: tasks that do not belong to any project.

A new account has a project marked **Sample** called **Weekend in Hangzhou**. You can look through it, but you cannot send messages there.

**The Chats tab** lists your chats with people and Agents, and your group chats. At the top are three rows: **New task**, **New group chat** and **Add contact**. To work with other people, start a group chat here. See [Chat and messages](/en/chat) and [Groups and collaboration](/en/groups).

### Show them together

To see projects and chats in one list, click **Sidebar layout** at the top right of the sidebar, or right-click an empty part of the sidebar, then choose **Projects and chats together**. The **Chats** section then sits below the projects, and the **+** on its heading offers **New group chat** and **Add contact**. Choose **Projects and chats separately** to bring back the two tabs.

## Search and commands

**Search...** at the top of the sidebar opens the quick switcher. You can also press ⌘O (Ctrl+O on Windows and Linux). Type a name to jump to a task, group, project or contact.

Type `>` first in the search box to switch to commands.

## Create an Agent

Each new task comes with a new Agent. A few other entry points create an Agent directly:

- **Create Agent** on a device section in **Contacts**.
- **New Agent in this group** in a group's member list. The new Agent joins that group.
- On Windows, the **File** menu at the top of the window → **New agent**.

For Agent settings and management, see [Engines and Agents](/en/agents).

## The top of a group chat

Open a group. The top right shows, from left to right:

- Icons of Apps pinned to this group, and **Group Apps**: open an App in this group, or pin an App to the group header.
- **Search this chat**.
- **Group folder**: files shared by the group; see [Group folder](/en/folder). In a one-to-one chat it is called **Folder**.
- **Members**: members, the announcement and group settings.
- **More actions**: the group's topic list and group info.

Pinned topics appear in a row of tabs above the messages, next to **Group**. Click one to switch to it. For how to open a topic and who gets notified, see [Topics](/en/topics).

A project group holds only you and your Agents. To work with other people, start a group chat in **Chats**.

## The phone layout

The bottom of the phone app has two tabs, **Messages** and **Partners**. You can also swipe left and right between them.

- **Me**: tap your own avatar at the top left of **Messages** or **Partners**.
- **Personal assistant**: once set up, it is the first row in **Messages**. Tap it to open a full page, then switch between **Chat**, **Activity**, **Tasks**, **Memory** and **Profile**.
- For groups and tasks on the phone, see [Manage tasks from your phone](/en/mobile).

## Replay the tour

On desktop, after the first sign-in setup, gugu asks **Want a quick look around?** Choose **Show me (1 min)** to be shown gugu, projects, chats, contacts, the library, Apps and settings in turn. Choose **Just start** to skip it.

To see it again later, click **Help & tour** on the left rail and choose **Start the tour over**. If you skipped or left the tour before the end, the menu also offers **Continue the tour**, which picks up from the step where you stopped.
